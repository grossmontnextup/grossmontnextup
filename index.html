If you've ever been in foster care, this page is for you.

Money for food, gas and books. People who know exactly which hoops to jump through. All you have to do is ask.

NextUp at Grossmont College is free, year-round support for current and former foster youth. No essay. No competition. Fill out one form and a real person calls you.

  • Hundreds of dollars in grant money every semester you're enrolled
  • Money to help cover the cost of books
  • Grocery, gas and Target cards
  • Emergency cash when a car breaks or a tooth aches
Send me the free zine Or call Sam: 619-644-3932

Read this far and we'll mail you one of our mini zines — on courage, happiness, or making smart decisions (28 larger pages, so not that mini). Free, no strings.

Eligible if you've been in foster care any time since your 13th birthday and you're under 26. Or if you belonged to any other college's NextUp program. Not sure? Ask us and we'll check for you.

How to pick the right college

A mostly unbiased guide

The college with the most "yes" answers wins. Here, every answer is yes.

  1. Can you get money for books and life totaling into the hundreds of dollars every semester you're enrolled in courses?

    Yes

  2. Can you get grocery store cards to help ensure you have food at home because, well, you've grown rather fond of eating?

    Yes

  3. Can you get bus and trolley support or gas cards to help save you money?

    Yes

  4. Can you get emergency grant money because cars break, teeth ache, apartment managers demand hefty deposits, and, well, you get the idea?

    Yes

  5. Can you get support to pay for prescription and over-the-counter medications at CVS?

    Yes

  6. Can you get support for prescription glasses, so you can read the white board from the back of the room and textbooks and screens up close?

    Yes

  7. Can you get a Target card for each of the three academic counseling appointments per semester?

    Yes

  8. Can you get a financial aid specialist dedicated to your success, to help ensure you get your money on time and that your questions get answered?

    Yes

  9. Can you meet with academic counselors who have been supporting foster youth in college since before you were in kindergarten?

    Yes

  10. Can you get housing within, say, 21 miles?

    Yes

  11. Do they have your major?

    Ask us
